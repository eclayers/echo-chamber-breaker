The Anti-Sycophant Epistemic Firewall for Amazon Bedrock & Alexa+

Modern frontier models are fine-tuned via RLHF to be polite, conversational, and accommodating. When users articulate polarized or unexamined convictions, standard chatbots become sycophantic yes-men ("You're completely right!"), compounding cognitive echo chambers.

Echo Chamber Breaker turns this dynamic upside down: it operates as a rigorous Socratic sparring partner. Using Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Amazon Bedrock, it charitably reconstructs the strongest possible opposing argument (Steelmanning), identifies cognitive blind spots, and logs intellectual humility in a tamper-proof append-only ledger.

⚖️ Philosophical Epistemic Framework
Rapoport's First Rule
"Re-express your target's position so clearly, vividly, and fairly that your target says: 'Thanks, I wish I'd thought of putting it that way.'"
Popperian Falsification
A belief only holds intellectual merit if the speaker can articulate what empirical evidence or discovery would lead them to revise it.
Alexa Voice Brevity (≤ 40 words)
Auditory cognition requires crisp, bounded summaries for Alexa voice users, while screens display multi-dimensional pillars.
